How to Report an ADR?

Important Notice:

Report all suspected reactions even if you are not certain that the drug caused it. Your data helps in making medicines safer.

  • Step 1: Patient Details
    Fill initials, age, and weight accurately to help identify patient profile.
  • Step 2: Medication
    Include brand name or generic name with specific dosage and route.
  • Step 3: Reaction
    Describe symptoms clearly and specify if hospitalization was required.
  • Step 4: Reporter
    Provide your name and profession for medical follow-up if needed.
